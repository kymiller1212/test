Common questions

Is ReadBuddy free?
Yes! ReadBuddy comes with 54 built-in stories that are completely free. To generate custom stories about your child's interests, you'll need to connect an API key from OpenAI or Anthropic (which have their own pricing, typically pennies per story).
What reading level is this for?
ReadBuddy has two reading levels: Level 1 (2nd grade, ~90 words per story) and Level 2 (3rd grade, ~140 words per story). Stories use age-appropriate vocabulary with built-in word banks and quizzes.
Is this designed for kids with dyslexia?
Yes. Every design choice — the Lexend font, adjustable letter and word spacing, warm background colors, reading ruler, and syllable helper — follows best practices for dyslexia-friendly reading. But any struggling reader can benefit from these features.
Where is my child's data stored?
When you sign in with Google, your progress is stored securely in the cloud and syncs across devices. Your API key is encrypted and never exposed to the browser after you enter it.
What do I need an API key for?
The API key powers the AI story generator. When your child types a topic like "dinosaurs" or "Minecraft," ReadBuddy uses OpenAI or Anthropic to write original stories at their reading level. Without a key, you still get all 54 built-in stories.
Does it work on phones and tablets?
Absolutely. ReadBuddy is mobile-first and works great on phones, tablets, and desktops. No app store download needed — it runs right in your browser.

How do I get an OpenAI key?
  1. Go to platform.openai.com
  2. Sign up or log in
  3. Click API Keys in the sidebar
  4. Click Create new secret key
  5. Copy and paste it above
How do I get an Anthropic key?
  1. Go to console.anthropic.com
  2. Sign up or log in
  3. Click API Keys
  4. Click Create Key
  5. Copy and paste it above

This lets ReadBuddy create stories about whatever your child is interested in. Your key stays on this device.

You'll still get 54 built-in stories, but won't be able to generate new ones.
